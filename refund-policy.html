Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Project deposit

A 50% deposit of the total agreed project fee is required before work begins. This confirms your booking and allows time and resources to be allocated to your project.

Deposit refunds

Once work has started, the 50% deposit is non-refundable. This applies if you cancel the project or decide not to proceed, including where you are dissatisfied with work completed to that point, because the deposit covers the time and work already invested in your project.

Final payment

The remaining 50% of the agreed project fee is due when the project is completed and before final delivery, launch, or transfer of completed project files, unless a written agreement states otherwise.

Changes to scope

Any work outside the agreed project scope may require a separate quote and can affect the project timeline and final cost. These changes will be discussed and agreed before the additional work begins.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your project payment schedule, please email info@rusman.gr.
