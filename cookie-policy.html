Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What cookies and storage are

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. This site also uses browser storage for similar settings. They help the website remember your choices and, if you agree, understand how visitors use the site.

Essential preferences

The cookie-consent setting records your cookie choice. The theme setting remembers whether you selected the light or dark theme. These are stored locally in your browser and are needed to respect your preferences.

Optional analytics

With your permission, Google Analytics uses the _ga and _ga_* cookies to measure page visits, device and browser information, approximate location, and interactions with the site. This helps improve the website. Analytics is not loaded until you accept it, and it is not used for advertising.

Optional visual effect preference

If you enable the seasonal particle effect, the particles-enabled preference is saved in browser storage. If browser storage is unavailable, it may be saved as a first-party cookie for up to one year.

Third-party services

Submitting the contact form sends the information you enter to Formspree. Formspree may process technical data needed to provide the service under its own privacy policy.

Manage your preferences

You can change your analytics choice at any time.
